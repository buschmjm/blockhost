Let friends join from anywhere

Players on your Wi-Fi can always use the local address. For anyone else, pick one of these. Try the router first; use the tunnel if the router can't do it.

1. Open the port on your router (UPnP)

Asks your router to forward UDP port 19132 to this computer, and keeps it open while Blockhost runs. Turning it off closes the port again. Works on most home routers; it can't work if your internet provider shares one public address between customers (CGNAT).

2. Use a free playit.gg tunnel

Works behind any router, including CGNAT. Blockhost downloads playit's agent and you link it to a free playit.gg account once. Players connect through playit's servers, so there's a little extra delay. playit.gg is a separate service with its own terms.

3. I set it up myself

Already forwarded the port by hand, or have your own domain? Enter the address players should use. This takes priority over 1 and 2 on the dashboard.

Check it works

Asks mcsrvstat.us, a public Minecraft status service, to reach your external address from the internet. Testing from your own network often fails even when friends can join, so this is the reliable check.
